\paragraph{A withdrawn claim.} An earlier draft attributed the \rung{r6} failure to \emph{endogenous
slack}, the policy's own volume evicting its future targets, citing $S(100)$ falling from $1.00$ to
$0.055$ (\texttt{wiki\_2019t}) and $0.135$ (\texttt{cluster50}). Those figures are correctly
computed but were measured on an \emph{unmetered} replay at zero threshold and fan-out 32, admitting
13{,}567{,}877 objects over 2M requests, $13.8\times$ the bar's 982{,}954, and so describe no arm
the Ladder scores. The table above repeats the measurement on the scored, byte-metered arm.
Independently, Stage~1's iso-bandwidth discipline is what prevents volume from being the operative
mechanism, so a volume-driven account is inconsistent with the design that produced the number.

\section{E. Learned-index error distributions}

\noindent Key-set provenance: \texttt{books}, \texttt{osm\_cellids} and \texttt{fb} are SOSD key
sets; \texttt{meta\_rprn} and \texttt{wiki\_2019t} are the trace identifier sets. Against binary
search alone, the field's customary comparison, the real-key rows of the main text's Table~4 read
$91.9$, $90.9$, $69.7$, $40.0$ and $22.7$\%, so the baseline correction is worth up to 290 points of
apparent capture (\texttt{wiki\_2019t}, $22.7\to-267.3$). Under a byte-matched leaf cap ($N/35$
rather than $N/16$) \texttt{books} reads $56.8$\% and \texttt{meta\_rprn} $27.9$\%, so only
\texttt{books} is claimed as a build.

\section{G. On the number of positives}

\noindent Across four settings and eight key sets the procedure returns one unambiguous \emph{build}
and one marginal. The property under test is not the rate of positives but whether the verdict
tracks a measured property of the input: capture is predicted by Section~E, measured independently
of the verdict. A bar tuned to produce positives would not exhibit that correspondence.
